How this works

The model is a scikit-learn LinearRegression fit on six features: square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, age, a location desirability score, and garage spaces. After training, the learned coefficients and intercept were exported as JSON and embedded directly in this page — the "AI" here is just the dot product of your inputs with those real learned weights, computed live in JavaScript.
